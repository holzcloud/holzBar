## Vendor claims vs independent facts
- Independent coverage found: only Product Hunt (80 upvotes, no formal reviews) and templated aggregators. No hands-on review. Everything above is vendor-stated.
- Vendor blog is marketing; its macOS 27 "test" (only Lounge "reliably hid items", Ice/Thaw/Hidden Bar/Barbee/Bartender "not reliably") is unverifiable and self-serving.
- Questionable claims about others: says Ice "requested Screen Recording" (Ice treats it as optional/limited mode, which the same table concedes); says Ice notch handling "only partial"; says Ice/Thaw/Barbee/Bartender "all request Screen Recording" while table says Thaw marks it Optional; cites a "medium-threat ... thin SEO footprint" dataset as evidence about Ice. Verify against Ice/Thaw sources before using.
- Internal contradiction: site says macOS 26+, changelog lists macOS 12/13+ minimum.
- "Entirely on your Mac" / private-leaning marketing sits next to a privacy policy that discloses analytics, crash reports and licence checks.
