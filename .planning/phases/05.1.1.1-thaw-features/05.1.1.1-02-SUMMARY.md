# Phase 05.1.1.1 Plan 02: item hints, keyboard and VoiceOver, rehide after the menu closes, change reveal Summary

A hotkey shows every menu bar item with a letter under it and opens the one typed; every way of opening an item now goes through one path; the Layout pane and the holzBar Shelf work from the keyboard with undo, redo and VoiceOver actions; opened items stay up to 30 s after their menu closes or open in place without showing; and items marked "Show When It Changes" show for 5 s when their title or value changes.

## What was done

### Task 1: open an item by letter, one open path (THAW-14) — c72045f
- `ItemHints` (Core, tested): letters by position, home row first, two-letter hints once the single letters run out (no hint starts another), matching of the typed letters.
- `ItemHintsPanel`: a key panel under the menu bar; typing a letter opens that item (hidden ones included), Escape and losing key status close it; VoiceOver reads "A, Wi-Fi".
- `HotkeyAction.showItemHints` (`ShowItemHints`, appended to the pinned list) and its recorder.
- The Shelf, the search, groups, hotkeys and intents call `ItemOpener.openItem`; `MenuBarItemActions` was removed.

### Task 2: keyboard, undo, VoiceOver (THAW-13) — 057f052
- `LayoutKeys` (Core, tested): arrows move focus, Option-Left/Right move one place, Command-1/2/3 set the section; on macOS 27 moves within a section beep and say why.
- `LayoutBarMoves` (in `LayoutBarPaddingView.swift`): move with undo registration, move to end, set section (both backends), undo and synchronous redo.
- `LayoutBarItemView`: focusable with a focus ring, key handling, Command-Z and Shift-Command-Z, button role, label "name, section", custom accessibility actions, announcements.
- Profiles: deleting is undoable with its binding and hotkey.
- holzBar Shelf: becomes key when opened by its hotkey; arrows, Return, Space and Escape; accessibility actions Open and Open Menu. Without Screen Recording it lists the items with a compact hint.

### Task 3: rehide after the menu closes, open in place, change reveal (THAW-12) — 7764130
- `MenuBarItemManager`: the rehide delay runs after the opened item's menu closes; Advanced pane "Hide opened items again after" 0…30 s with an annotation; `tempShowInterval` is clamped to 30.
- "Open hidden items in the menu bar" (`OpenHiddenItemsInMenuBar`, default on): off presses the item in place.
- `ChangeReveal` (Core, tested with binary-exact times) and `ItemChangeWatcher` (AXObserver per app for title and value changes of marked items); `RevealOnChangeItems` (string array); "Show When It Changes" in the layout item's context menu.
- README rows and features.

## Deviations from Plan

### Auto-fixed Issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] Redo lost when registered in a Task**
- **Found during:** Task 2
- **Issue:** A redo registered from an asynchronous move is not recorded as the redo of the undo being performed.
- **Fix:** The undo handler registers the redo synchronously before it starts the move (`registersUndo` parameter).
- **Commit:** 057f052

**2. [Rule 2] Edit-menu key equivalents while holzBar is hidden**
- **Found during:** Task 2
- **Issue:** As an agent app, holzBar's Edit menu did not deliver Command-Z to the Layout pane.
- **Fix:** The Edit menu's items get `allowsKeyEquivalentWhenHidden`; the item view also handles Command-Z and Shift-Command-Z itself.
- **Commit:** 057f052

**3. [Rule 2] Shelf without Screen Recording**
- **Found during:** Task 2
- **Issue:** Without Screen Recording the keyboard-driven Shelf showed nothing to move through.
- **Fix:** It lists the items with a compact Screen Recording hint (plan 03 adds app icons).
- **Commit:** 057f052

**4. Layout moves in the padding view**
- `LayoutBarMoves` lives in `LayoutBarPaddingView.swift`, next to the drop code it shares, instead of a new file.

**5. Every layout item is focusable**
- Not only movable ones, so VoiceOver and the keyboard reach every item and can read why it does not move.

- "Open an item by letter": every item shows a letter under the menu bar; typing one opens that item's menu (hidden ones included), Escape closes without opening; VoiceOver reads each hint "A, Wi-Fi". The Shelf, the search and a group still open items as before.
- macOS 26 Layout pane: Tab to an item (focus ring), arrows move focus, Option-Right moves it one place, Command-2 moves it to Hidden, Command-Z puts it back and Shift-Command-Z redoes; a mouse drop is undone the same way. macOS 27: Option-Right beeps and says why; Command-1/2/3 change its section and undo.
- Delete a profile and press Command-Z: it returns with its binding and hotkey; the bar does not move.
- VoiceOver: layout items read name and section and offer the move actions; Shelf items offer Open and Open Menu. The Shelf opened by its hotkey works with arrows, Return and Escape.
- With the delay at 10 s an item opened from the Shelf stays 10 s after its menu closes, on 26 and 27; with "Open hidden items in the menu bar" off its menu opens without the item appearing.
- Mark a counter or timer app "Show When It Changes": it shows for 5 s when its title changes, at most every 30 s, never in Zen mode; note which apps post no change.
