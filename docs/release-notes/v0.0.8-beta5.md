## holzBar 0.0.8 beta 5

The fifth beta of 0.0.8 "Automation". It finishes the per-item conditions, tidies Menu Bar Layout and fixes the glyphs on its section strips. Please report problems on the [issue tracker](https://github.com/holzcloud/holzBar/issues). Coming from 0.0.7, the notes of [beta 1](https://github.com/holzcloud/holzBar/releases/tag/v0.0.8-beta1), [beta 2](https://github.com/holzcloud/holzBar/releases/tag/v0.0.8-beta2), [beta 3](https://github.com/holzcloud/holzBar/releases/tag/v0.0.8-beta3) and [beta 4](https://github.com/holzcloud/holzBar/releases/tag/v0.0.8-beta4) describe everything else that is new.

### ✨ Highlights
- **Show Only When… from the item itself**: right-click an item in **Menu Bar Layout** (VoiceOver has the same action) and choose **Show Only When…** to make its rule: the item is shown while the conditions hold and hidden in the Hidden or Always-Hidden section while they do not. It is the editor of the Automation rules with the action fixed, so the rule also shows in the Automation pane. An item has one rule; **Edit Rule…** and **Remove Rule** are in the same menu. An item that a rule controls wears a small eye badge, and its tooltip names the rule.
- **Profiles leave controlled items alone**: applying a profile no longer moves an item that a rule controls; the rule wins. The Profiles section says how many items that are. Restoring a snapshot still restores everything: it is your own action.

### 🔧 Changed
- **Menu Bar Layout shows the sections first.** Below the hint come the Visible, Hidden and Always Hidden bars, then Tidy Up, Profiles, Groups, Spacers, Widgets and Drag in the Menu Bar, and **Layout History** last.
- **Layout History is folded away until you open it**, and it keeps the **newest 10 snapshots** by default. Choose from 1 to 100 with **Snapshots to keep**; older ones are deleted at once. Starred snapshots always stay and are not counted. This replaces the earlier rule of one snapshot per day.

### 🛠 Fixed
- **The glyphs on the section strips of Menu Bar Layout are readable.** Widgets and the pictures of items were drawn in black, which is unreadable on the strip's dark colour. They are drawn in white on a dark strip and in black on a bright one, as the strip's colour requires.

### ⚠️ Known issues
- A rule that controls an item moves it only when its condition changes, after a hold of three seconds. If you move such an item by hand, it stays there until the condition changes. When the condition holds again, the item goes back to the visible bar, not to the section you had put it in.
- On macOS 27 a rule for one item moves all items of its application, and the order within a section is up to macOS.
- The known issues of [beta 4](https://github.com/holzcloud/holzBar/releases/tag/v0.0.8-beta4) still apply.

### 📦 Install
Update an installed holzBar (this is a beta; it is on the beta channel only):
```sh
brew update && brew upgrade --cask holzbar@beta
```

If you installed the stable `holzbar`, switch to the beta channel first: `brew uninstall --cask holzbar`, then use the commands below.

New install (quit the original Ice, Thaw, Bartender or Hidden Bar first if one of them is running):
```sh
brew tap holzcloud/holzbar https://github.com/holzcloud/holzBar
brew trust --cask holzcloud/holzbar/holzbar@beta
brew install --cask holzcloud/holzbar/holzbar@beta
```

If macOS says holzBar can't be opened, take it out of quarantine and open it again:
```sh
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/holzBar.app
```

This release is signed with the same certificate as 0.0.6, 0.0.7 and the earlier 0.0.8 betas, so updating keeps the Accessibility and Screen Recording permissions. Coming from 0.0.5, follow "Install and update" in the [0.0.6 notes](https://github.com/holzcloud/holzBar/releases/tag/v0.0.6).

holzBar runs on macOS 14 Sonoma and later, up to macOS 27. Only macOS 26 and 27 are tested.
