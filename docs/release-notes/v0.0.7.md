## holzBar 0.0.7

A bugfix release: the fixes from a full bug and security audit, settings sync removed from the app, a menu bar and holzBar Shelf that follow Reduce Transparency and Increase Contrast, and two fixes for macOS 26. It adds no new features. This is the first stable release since 0.0.6. If you come from 0.0.6, the details of each step are in the notes of the betas: [beta 1](https://github.com/holzcloud/holzBar/releases/tag/v0.0.7-beta1), [beta 2](https://github.com/holzcloud/holzBar/releases/tag/v0.0.7-beta2), [beta 3](https://github.com/holzcloud/holzBar/releases/tag/v0.0.7-beta3) and [beta 4](https://github.com/holzcloud/holzBar/releases/tag/v0.0.7-beta4).

### ✨ Highlights
- **A full bug and security audit.** Beta 2 fixed 93 of its 112 findings and part of one more, including all three rated high (none was critical). holzBar ships as a single executable with no helper process or nested code, and release builds carry no entitlement at all.
- **Settings sync is gone from holzBar.** A redesigned sync is planned for 1.0.0. **Export…** and **Import…** in **Settings → Advanced** stay, so you can still move your settings to another Mac. Nothing stored was deleted.
- **System Glass and the holzBar Shelf follow Reduce Transparency and Increase Contrast.** With either on in System Settings, Accessibility, Display, they are drawn opaque with a visible border, and the change applies at once.
- **Fixes for macOS 26 found in everyday use (beta 4).** The spinner after **Apply** in **Menu bar item spacing** no longer runs on behind a hidden alert, and items of other apps keep their app, without holzBar re-reading the menu bar every minute.

### ⚠️ Known issues
- **Not checked on real macOS 26 and 27 systems by the maintainer.** The opaque glass and Shelf, the spacing Apply and the item names have unit tests and the app type-checks and builds in CI, but how they look and behave on a real Mac still needs hands-on testing. Please report anything that looks wrong on the [issue tracker](https://github.com/holzcloud/holzBar/issues).
- **Native support for macOS 27's own overflow button is not part of 0.0.7.** holzBar does not cooperate with that button yet; it is planned for a later release.
- **System items keep the old spacing until your next login** after you change the menu bar item spacing (the clock, Wi-Fi, battery); holzBar does not force Control Center and SystemUIServer to quit.
- The macOS 27 Liquid Glass slider is not followed: macOS offers no public signal for it.
- **Macs still on 0.0.6 or 0.0.7 beta 1 keep syncing** with each other through the sync folder, and a Mac on 0.0.7 no longer takes part. Update them as well, or use **Export…** and **Import…**.
- The other known issues of 0.0.6 still apply.

### 📦 Install
Update an installed holzBar:
```sh
brew update && brew upgrade --cask holzbar
```

New install (quit the original Ice, Thaw, Bartender or Hidden Bar first if one of them is running):
```sh
brew tap holzcloud/holzbar https://github.com/holzcloud/holzBar
brew trust --cask holzcloud/holzbar/holzbar
brew install --cask holzbar
```

If macOS says holzBar can't be opened, take it out of quarantine and open it again:
```sh
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/holzBar.app
```

This release is signed with the same certificate as 0.0.6 and the 0.0.7 betas, so updating keeps the Accessibility and Screen Recording permissions. Coming from 0.0.5, follow "Install and update" in the [0.0.6 notes](https://github.com/holzcloud/holzBar/releases/tag/v0.0.6).

holzBar runs on macOS 14 Sonoma and later, up to macOS 27.
