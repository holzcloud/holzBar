## holzBar 0.0.9

The first stable release of 0.0.9: a command palette, an AppleScript dictionary, moods (saved looks of the menu bar), grid and column layouts for the holzBar Shelf, opt-in usage suggestions, sound output and Bluetooth as rule conditions, and a redesigned Settings window with a Modules pane. It is the sum of the betas 1 and 2 and the work since. Coming from 0.0.8, everything below is new. **Most of it is a Preview: built and unit tested, but not yet tested on a Mac.** A Preview badge in the app marks the features that have one, until each has been confirmed on its own. Please report problems on the [issue tracker](https://github.com/holzcloud/holzBar/issues).

### ✨ Highlights
- **A new Settings window**: the sidebar has three groups (Menu Bar Layout, Menu Bar Appearance and Automation; Modules; General, Hotkeys, Advanced and About), the window ground falls from a calm colour to a deeper one with two soft glows under the glass, and Settings can shrink to 760 by 540 points. Widgets and Drag in the Menu Bar moved from Menu Bar Layout to the new **Modules** pane.
- **Command palette** (Preview): set a hotkey in **Settings → Hotkeys** and a Spotlight-like panel opens. Type to find an action, a layout profile, a mood, a rule or a menu bar item; Return runs it, Escape closes it. Starting with `>` leaves out the menu bar items. It keeps no history and needs no permission; with the Touch ID lock on it asks first, because it lists hidden items.
- **AppleScript dictionary** (Preview): show, hide and toggle a section, apply a profile, turn rules and Zen mode on or off, and read the profile and rule names. Commands take the same decision as `holzbar://` URL commands: lasting changes ask first. A script cannot create or change rules, profiles, scripts or settings.
- **Moods** (Preview): save the current look of the menu bar under a name, switch to it in one click, from the palette, or with a rule ("switch to a mood"), for example by time of day or when a display is connected. At most twelve.
- **Usage suggestions** (Preview, off by default): after you turn it on, holzBar counts how often you open each item (per day, 30 days, in one file on this Mac). After 14 days it suggests hiding items you did not open for 14 days. **View Data** shows everything stored and **Forget Everything** deletes it.

### 🆕 New
- **Modules**: a pane in Settings that lists the optional modules, each off until you switch it on. A module's settings open under its row only while it is on. Widgets and Drag in the Menu Bar are the first two.
- **Sound output and Bluetooth as rule conditions** (Preview): "the Mac plays sound through …" needs no permission. "A Bluetooth device is connected" asks macOS for the Bluetooth permission only when you add such a condition.
- **holzBar Shelf layouts** (Preview): a row, a grid or a column, and the Shelf fades in (never with Reduce Motion on). Up and down arrows move the highlight in a grid and a column.
- **Put Back by a Rule** (Preview): when you move an item that a rule controls and the rule does not allow it, holzBar puts it back at the next evaluation and says so in a section at the top of Menu Bar Layout, with **Edit Rule…** and **OK**. holzBar remembers the section the item had, so it returns there and not to the visible bar, also after a restart. VoiceOver announces a put-back.
- **Permission pills**: Advanced, the permissions window and the Screen Recording hint show "Needs <permission>" or "Permission Granted" as a small label, read by VoiceOver before the Grant or Revoke button.
- **Key caps**: a hotkey you set is drawn as key caps in the hotkey recorder, and VoiceOver reads it once as a whole shortcut.
- **Keep the Mac awake** from a hotkey and from the command palette.

### 🔧 Changed
- **The strips of Menu Bar Layout look like the menu bar of macOS**: a translucent glass band in the colour of the real bar, with a soft sheen at the top, instead of a flat dark surface.
- **Lasting confirmations are sheets of one design**: deleting a rule, removing a widget, deleting a profile, deleting all snapshots, resetting the appearance, revoking a permission, restoring a layout and allowing a script. For a destructive sheet Return is Cancel and Escape cancels; Restore is the one confirming sheet where Return restores. Revoking also offers **Open System Settings**.
- **An item that a rule controls and that you moved by hand goes back** at the next evaluation of its rule, instead of staying where you put it. While the rule shows the item, a move by hand is kept as the section the item has when shown, as long as it is more visible than the hiding section.
- The keyboard and VoiceOver [checklist](https://github.com/holzcloud/holzBar/blob/main/docs/accessibility-checklist.md) covers the Modules pane, the confirmation sheets, key caps and permission pills.
- New texts exist in English, German, French, Italian and Romansh; the French, Italian and Romansh texts are machine-made and not yet checked by a native speaker.

### ⚠️ Known issues
- **Not tested on a Mac:** the Preview features above and the new look of Settings (the Modules pane, the sidebar groups, the window ground, the confirmation sheets, key caps and permission pills). Report what does not work.
- A rule cannot use Energy Mode: macOS has no public interface for it. Low Power Mode works as before.
- Smooth show and hide covers only the holzBar Shelf; the bar itself does not animate yet.
- Replacement items for Apple's items on macOS 27 are not part of this release.
- The French, Italian and Romansh texts are machine-made and not yet reviewed.
- The accessibility changes were not checked with VoiceOver, Full Keyboard Access and Voice Control on a Mac; the [checklist](https://github.com/holzcloud/holzBar/blob/main/docs/accessibility-checklist.md) lists what to check.
- The other known issues of [0.0.8](https://github.com/holzcloud/holzBar/releases/tag/v0.0.8) still apply.

### 📦 Install
Update an installed holzBar:
```sh
brew update && brew upgrade --cask holzbar
```

If you installed the beta channel (`holzbar@beta`), switch to the stable one: `brew uninstall --cask holzbar@beta`, then use the commands below.

New install (quit the original Ice, Thaw, Bartender or Hidden Bar first if one of them is running):
```sh
brew tap holzcloud/holzbar https://github.com/holzcloud/holzBar
brew trust --cask holzcloud/holzbar/holzbar
brew install --cask holzcloud/holzbar/holzbar
```

If macOS says holzBar can't be opened, take it out of quarantine and open it again:
```sh
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/holzBar.app
```

This release is signed with the same certificate as 0.0.6, 0.0.7, 0.0.8 and the 0.0.9 betas, so updating keeps the Accessibility and Screen Recording permissions. Coming from 0.0.5, follow "Install and update" in the [0.0.6 notes](https://github.com/holzcloud/holzBar/releases/tag/v0.0.6).

holzBar runs on macOS 14 Sonoma and later, up to macOS 27. Only macOS 26 and 27 are tested.
