## holzBar 0.0.8 beta 1

The first beta of 0.0.8 "Automation". It adds rules that change the menu bar by themselves, a history of your layout, a lock for the hidden items and a redesigned look for the settings. **Every new feature is a Preview: built and unit tested, but not yet tested on a real Mac by the maintainer.** Each one carries a **Preview** badge in the app. Please try them and report what does not work on the [issue tracker](https://github.com/holzcloud/holzBar/issues).

### ✨ Highlights
- **Automation** (new pane in Settings): rules of the kind "when these things are true, do this". Conditions: power source and battery level, Low Power Mode, an app running or in front, a time of day, a display connected, Wi-Fi or Ethernet or offline or a hotspot or a VPN, the name of the Wi-Fi network (asks for Location Services only when you add that condition, and only reads the network name) and a script. Actions: apply a layout profile, show a section, turn Zen mode on or off, keep the Mac awake, run a script, or show one item only while the condition holds. A rule acts once when it becomes true and, if you want, brings the previous state back when it ends. Nothing is observed unless an enabled rule needs it, and nothing polls.
- **Focus filter**: add the holzBar filter to a Focus in System Settings to apply a layout profile while that Focus is on.
- **Layout History**: automatic snapshots of your arrangement, with a preview of what a restore changes and an undo. Local files on this Mac only.
- **Lock hidden items**: Touch ID or your password before hidden items are shown, with a minute of grace. Your own safety rules (low battery, offline, rules, items you chose) do not ask. Turning the lock off asks too. In **Settings → Advanced**.
- **A new look for the settings**: glass groups on a calm ground with two soft glows, and a sidebar in two groups.

### 🆕 New
- **Tidy Up assistant**: proposes which items to hide from static facts only (system extras, the app's category, a short list of known helpers). It is offered once after the first launch and any time from **Menu Bar Layout**. You decide item by item, and a snapshot is kept first.
- **Share a profile** as a small validated file (only the apps you leave checked and their sections), and import one after a look. Double-clicking such a file in Finder is not set up yet; use **Import Profile…** in Menu Bar Layout.
- **Copy Diagnostics** in About: a report of versions and counts that you read first and copy yourself. No names of items, apps, profiles or networks; nothing is sent.
- **Scripts** from the Scripts folder as a rule condition or action: only after you allowed the exact content of each file, checked again before every run, with no shell and no arguments. They never come in through an imported settings file or a URL.
- **Revoke** each permission from **Settings → Advanced**.
- **The Menu Bar Layout pane works without Screen Recording** and shows app icons until you allow it.
- **Pinned system items** (macOS 26 and earlier): profiles and restored layouts leave the clock, battery, Wi-Fi, Control Center and sound where they are.
- **URL commands and Shortcuts for rules**: `holzbar://automation/enable/<name>` and `/disable/<name>` ask first; Shortcuts can turn a rule on or off and list the rules that are active.
- A **keyboard and VoiceOver checklist** in `docs/accessibility-checklist.md`. holzBar makes no accessibility claim until it was run.

### 🔧 Changed
- Every new text exists in English, German, French, Italian and Romansh. The French, Italian and Romansh texts are machine-made and not yet checked by a native speaker.

### ⚠️ Known issues
- **Nothing in this beta was tested on a real Mac by the maintainer.** The Focus filter in particular depends on macOS calling it, which has been reported not to work on macOS 26.5; if the filter never applies your profile, it is a limit of macOS and not of your setup.
- The older "show hidden items on low battery / when offline" settings are unchanged and not yet rules of the new engine.
- Rules cannot yet be exported with a script, by design; the other rules are exported and imported with your settings.
- A VPN is seen only when the traffic goes through a tunnel; a VPN that routes only some networks is not.
- On macOS 27 a rule that shows one item moves all items of its app.
- Camera and microphone as conditions, widgets, the AppleScript dictionary, animations, profile hooks and settings sync are planned for later releases.
- The other known issues of 0.0.7 still apply.

### 📦 Install
Update an installed holzBar (this is a beta; Homebrew installs the newest version of the cask):
```sh
brew update && brew upgrade --cask holzbar
```

New install (quit the original Ice, Thaw, Bartender or Hidden Bar first if one of them is running):
```sh
brew tap holzcloud/holzbar https://github.com/holzcloud/holzBar
brew trust --cask holzcloud/holzbar/holzbar
brew install --cask holzbar
```

If macOS says holzBar can't be opened, take it out of quarantine and open it again:
```sh
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/holzBar.app
```

This release is signed with the same certificate as 0.0.6 and 0.0.7, so updating keeps the Accessibility and Screen Recording permissions.

holzBar runs on macOS 14 Sonoma and later, up to macOS 27. Only macOS 26 and 27 are tested.
