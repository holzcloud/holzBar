## holzBar 0.0.7 beta 2

The fixes from a full bug and security audit. Everything new in 0.0.6 is described in the [0.0.6 notes](https://github.com/holzcloud/holzBar/releases/tag/v0.0.6).

### ✨ Highlights
holzBar went through a full bug and security audit. This beta fixes 93 of its 112 findings and part of one more, including all three rated high (none was critical); the 18 still open are rated low. holzBar now ships as a single executable: the menu bar item helper service is gone, and on macOS 26 holzBar finds each item's app itself. Release builds carry no entitlement at all any more, and every release now comes with SLSA Build Level 3 provenance in addition to GitHub's attestation, so you can check that a download was built from this repository's tag.

### 🆕 New
- **holzBar asks before settings sync replaces anything.** When you turn sync on or change the folder and it holds another Mac's different settings, or when both Macs changed their settings, holzBar asks "Which settings should holzBar use?": **Use Settings from Sync Folder** restarts holzBar with them, **Keep This Mac's Settings** replaces the ones in the folder. Until you answer, nothing is overwritten. When both Macs changed, choose **Choose Settings…** in the holzBar menu or in **Settings → Advanced**.
- **A dot on holzBar's icon while the microphone or camera is in use (macOS 27).** While holzBar hides items on macOS 27, Control Centre does not show its indicator. holzBar now marks its own icon instead: orange for the microphone, green for a camera. It needs no permission and is on by default in **Settings → General**. With **Show holzBar icon** off, the icon appears while it carries the dot.
- **Replace a hotkey combination that is already in use.** If you record a combination another holzBar hotkey uses, holzBar asks whether to use it here instead (**Replace** or Cancel), in **Settings → Hotkeys** and for item hotkeys in the Layout pane. Before, it saved a hotkey that never worked.
- **The Split shape works on macOS 27** instead of falling back to the full shape.

### 🛠 Fixed
#### Menu bar items and sections
- **Items keep their section more reliably.** The second and later untitled items of an app find their saved section again, outdated saved entries no longer move items back into old sections, and on macOS 27 several items of one app no longer share one entry.
- **Rehiding a temporarily shown item works when the item beside it is gone.** Before, it failed on every try and held up other moves.
- **New items that could not be placed are placed on a later pass**, and a restore stops as soon as you arrange items yourself instead of undoing your change.
- **"Keep Live Activities visible" and the placement of new items no longer fight over the same item**, and an app can no longer keep its item out of Hidden by putting "LiveActivit" in its title.
- **Automatic rehide:** the **Focused app** strategy respects **Automatically rehide**, holzBar's own activation no longer counts as a focus change, and **Smart** rehide, the default, now works without Screen Recording.
- **"Show When It Changes"** also watches an item that was not found the first time, and again after its app relaunches.
- **Application menus** are no longer hidden after the section was already hidden again, and opening or closing Settings while they are hidden no longer confuses holzBar.
- **Section dividers** drop the drag marker after a Command-drag ends.

#### Shelf and search
- **The holzBar Shelf no longer jumps** after it opens, and closing it while it is still loading closes it for good.
- **An item image that cannot be captured** no longer stops all item images from updating, or the search and the Shelf from opening, until holzBar quits. The search now opens at once and fills in images as they arrive.
- **The search no longer opens after you closed it** while it was loading, and the arrow and Return keys no longer break typing with an input method (Japanese, Chinese, …).
- **With "Open hidden items in the menu bar" off**, a hidden item's menu stays open, and the item no longer appears in the menu bar and gets clicked again.

#### macOS 26
- **Event timeouts work.** holzBar's time limits for moving and clicking items never fired, so one lost event hid the pointer and stopped moving, clicking and showing on hover until holzBar quit. holzBar now gives up after about two seconds and everything comes back, and the Shelf opens after at most a second. This also applies to macOS 14 and 15.
- **Caps Lock no longer stalls holzBar.** With Caps Lock on, every move and click waited indefinitely and ran later. On every macOS version, Option-click and Control-click on holzBar's icon and on empty menu bar space now also work with Caps Lock on.
- **No freezes when apps launch or quit** while the Shelf or the Layout pane reads items, and one slow app no longer stalls every read.
- **A newly launched app's item is recognised** without waiting up to 30 seconds.

#### macOS 27
- **Applying a layout profile no longer wipes the macOS 27 layout.** A profile saved before macOS 27 made every hidden item visible; now it leaves the layout alone, and apps a profile doesn't know keep their section.
- **Clicking the clock** while a banner was showing no longer cancels the dialog in front or ends fullscreen, and Notification Center opens on the first click.
- **Clicking an item in the Shelf** right after opening it no longer opens another item's menu, and a Shelf icon no longer shows another app's glyph. Item icons are photographed once more after the update.
- **Menu bar apps without a Dock icon** that start after holzBar are no longer kept hidden or squashed, and apps hidden for the notch come back when the reveal that crowded the bar ends.
- **Item icons follow a light/dark switch**, and a failed hiding of items is retried.
- **Clicks on the clock, battery, Wi-Fi and Control Centre are no longer held back** while holzBar starts watching a newly launched app, while a question is open in Settings, or while holzBar's Control-click menu is open.

#### Settings sync
- **Sync no longer overwrites other Macs' settings.** Turning sync on, every launch and answering "Later" used to write this Mac's settings over the folder's.
- **The other macOS version's layout is kept.** Between a macOS 26 Mac and a macOS 27 Mac, applying synced settings deleted the layout of the other version.
- **holzBar's own placement of new items no longer counts as a change.** It no longer turns **Restart** into **Choose Settings…** or shows a hint on your other Macs, and a Mac on macOS 26 and one on macOS 27 with the same settings are not asked which settings to use when they join. Neither does a Command-click in the menu bar that moves nothing.
- **Another Mac's menu bar arrangement is kept.** An updated Mac no longer writes its layout over the one a Mac of the same macOS version arranged, also one still on 0.0.7 beta 1, unless you rearranged this Mac's menu bar; then holzBar asks first. **Keep This Mac's Settings** keeps the other Mac's arrangement when you did not rearrange this Mac's, and this Mac takes it with the next restart.
- **Restart right after you rearranged the menu bar** on macOS 26 asks which settings to use instead of losing the move.
- **A sync file over 1 MB is left alone.** Another Mac no longer writes over it, and holzBar never writes one itself.
- **Launch never waits for the cloud.** Reading the sync file runs off the main thread; at launch holzBar waits at most a second and never for a file that is only online.
- **A sync folder on a network share is used only while the share is mounted.** holzBar no longer mounts it itself and blocks while that times out; it picks up again once the share is back.
- **Macs copied with Migration Assistant, a restore or a clone** get their own sync identity and see each other's changes.

#### Hotkeys
- **A hotkey stays registered** when its recorder goes away while recording, only one recorder records at a time, and Return dismisses a hotkey alert.
- **A hotkey row always shows its stored combination** and can clear it. A duplicate stored combination is dropped at launch; the first hotkey keeps it.
- **"Show the hidden section for a moment"** restarts its delay on repeated presses and no longer hides a section you showed by hand.

#### Appearance
- **holzBar recognises fullscreen spaces.** It never recognised one, on any macOS version, and some other system spaces counted as fullscreen. The bar's look, the Shelf, show on hover and hiding the application menus now follow fullscreen as intended.
- **A dynamic appearance** draws the other mode's style, and **Hold to Preview** shows it; **System Glass** follows light/dark switches.
- **An unknown shape, end cap or black-background value** in imported or synced settings no longer resets the whole appearance, and a custom icon that cannot be decoded falls back to holzBar's icon instead of an empty slot.
- **Backspace and Escape** act on the selected gradient stop only in the gradient picker, and distributing stops keeps the selected stop selected.

#### Layout pane
- **Rows keep updating** after a drag into another row or a cancelled drag, and keyboard and VoiceOver moves keep the focus on the item.
- **macOS 27:** a drag within one row no longer shows an order macOS does not keep.
- **macOS 26:** the unresponsive-app warning checks the item's own app, not Control Center.
- **Deleting a group** also deletes its custom image.

#### Stability
- **The alerts in Settings, the sync question and the "Not enough room" dialog no longer pause holzBar** while they are open; hover and the Shelf keep working.
- **Revoked permissions are noticed** when holzBar becomes active, instead of still showing "Permission Granted".
- **Before setup**, Shortcuts actions say holzBar is not ready, and opening Settings shows the permissions window.
- **An app that only has the name of a menu bar manager** is no longer force-quit.
- **With the pointer on the top row of a display**, holzBar no longer takes it for another display.

### 🔄 Changed
- **Alerts in Settings appear as sheets** on the Settings window: importing, an export error, an item that cannot be moved and the sync question.
- **Settings changed on another Mac show a quiet hint** instead of a dialog: the holzBar menu and **Settings → Advanced** show "Settings changed on another Mac" with **Restart**. If this Mac changed too, it reads **Choose Settings…**.
- **No helper process.** The menu bar item service is gone. On macOS 26 holzBar finds each item's app itself, on a background queue with time limits, and continues a lookup that ran out of time. A menu bar item that two apps claim belongs to neither.

### 🔒 Security
- **Release builds no longer let a debugger attach.** 0.0.7 beta 1 and every earlier release carried `com.apple.security.get-task-allow`. It let any program running as your user attach a debugger to holzBar and run code with its Accessibility and Screen Recording permissions. This release carries no entitlement at all, and CI, the release workflow and `Scripts/install.sh` fail if a build has one.
- **No nested code.** `Contents/MacOS/holzBar` is the only executable in the app. On macOS 26 the old helper service ran with holzBar's permissions, and holzBar never checked it, so a copied app with a replaced service kept them. CI and the release fail on any other code in the bundle.
- **The release checks its own signature.** It fails unless the app is signed with holzBar's certificate (SHA-256 `e55f0df15060b8c06c6842ccee85e6bc9f86cbbda3bd408abf991457840b1d95`) and its designated requirement; it never publishes a build signed ad hoc.
- **Provenance you can pin.** Besides GitHub's attestation, the release carries `holzBar-0.0.7-beta2.intoto.jsonl` (SLSA Build Level 3). Both can be checked against the release workflow and this tag:
  ```sh
  gh attestation verify holzBar-0.0.7-beta2.zip -R holzcloud/holzBar \
    --signer-workflow holzcloud/holzBar/.github/workflows/release.yml \
    --source-ref refs/tags/v0.0.7-beta2 --deny-self-hosted-runners
  slsa-verifier verify-artifact holzBar-0.0.7-beta2.zip \
    --provenance-path holzBar-0.0.7-beta2.intoto.jsonl \
    --source-uri github.com/holzcloud/holzBar --source-tag v0.0.7-beta2
  ```
  More in [docs/signing.md](https://github.com/holzcloud/holzBar/blob/main/docs/signing.md).
- **A hardened release pipeline.** Building, signing and publishing run in separate jobs, and only the signing job sees the certificate. Every action is pinned by commit SHA, except the SLSA generator, which has to be referenced by its release tag (v2.1.0), and OpenSSF Scorecard, CodeQL and Dependabot watch the repository. The signing secrets are still repository secrets for now; [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/holzcloud/holzBar/blob/main/SECURITY.md) lists that risk.
- **macOS 26: another app can no longer make the camera and microphone indicator hideable** by claiming its menu bar item. The camera, microphone and FaceTime indicators and the screen recording stop button are never hidden.
- **`holzbar://search` and `holzbar://settings` are ignored while Zen mode is on**, so a URL cannot show hidden items while you share your screen.
- **Fewer screen captures.** The Shelf no longer captures the screen on macOS 27 or while it is hidden, and on macOS 27 the menu bar is captured only while it is on screen.
- **macOS 27: other apps can no longer crash holzBar** with an invalid item frame, or make hidden items flash with a fake theme-change notification.

### ⚠️ Known issues
- **Settings sync may ask once after the update.** Each Mac joins the sync folder once more; if the Macs' settings differ, holzBar asks which to use one time. The menu bar layout of a Mac that already synced does not count: it takes the folder's layout for its macOS version.
- **Update every Mac that syncs.** A Mac still on 0.0.7 beta 1 keeps writing its settings over the folder's and applies the folder's at launch without asking. Updated Macs keep its menu bar layout, but while a Mac of the same macOS version is still on beta 1, rearranging the menu bar on an updated Mac asks which settings to use. Until every Mac is updated, don't change or recreate the sync folder: a beta 1 Mac of the other macOS version can then lose its layout or get an older one back.
- **A large custom holzBar icon** (a few hundred KB) can push the settings over the sync file's 1 MB limit. Sync then stops without saying so: no Mac writes over or applies such a file, and this Mac does not write its settings. Choose a smaller icon to sync again.
- **The capture dot does not cover screen recording.** On macOS 27 Control Centre's indicator is still hidden while holzBar hides items.
- **On macOS 26, applying a new item spacing** can skip an app whose items holzBar could not attribute; quit and reopen that app.
- **Several fixes still need hands-on testing**, above all on macOS 27 (the clock and Shelf clicks, the notch, the capture dot, the Split shape) and in fullscreen, which holzBar recognises for the first time. Please report anything that behaves differently from 0.0.7 beta 1 on the [issue tracker](https://github.com/holzcloud/holzBar/issues).
- The other known issues of 0.0.6 still apply.

### 📦 Install
Update an installed holzBar:
```sh
brew update && brew upgrade --cask holzbar
```

New install (quit the original Ice, Thaw, Bartender or Hidden Bar first if one of them is running):
```sh
brew tap holzcloud/holzbar https://github.com/holzcloud/holzBar
brew trust --cask holzcloud/holzbar/holzbar
brew install --cask holzbar
```

If macOS says holzBar can't be opened, take it out of quarantine and open it again:
```sh
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/holzBar.app
```

This release is signed with the same certificate as 0.0.6 and 0.0.7 beta 1, so updating keeps the Accessibility and Screen Recording permissions. Coming from 0.0.5, follow "Install and update" in the [0.0.6 notes](https://github.com/holzcloud/holzBar/releases/tag/v0.0.6).

holzBar runs on macOS 14 Sonoma and later, up to macOS 27.
